What is Decisio?

Decisio is a decision-making app for iOS that helps you structure complex choices and rank options on real criteria — not gut feel and not pros/cons lists. It runs the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), the same multi-criteria method used by NASA, Boeing, and the World Bank, in a guided 5–10 minute flow on your phone.

Where most decision-making apps use simple weighted scoring (every criterion treated as equally important), Decisio compares your criteria pairwise on the Saaty 1-to-9 scale and flags contradictions in your own reasoning via a consistency ratio. AI assistance proposes criteria, suggests options, and challenges your top pick as a Devil's Advocate.

Common questions

What is the best decision-making app in 2026?

It depends on what you're deciding. For structured personal decisions (job offers, big purchases, relocations) Decisio is the only iOS app implementing real AHP pairwise comparisons with consistency-ratio validation and an AI Devil's Advocate. For team voting a tool like ChoiceMap fits better. For spreadsheet-heavy analysis, Excel or Google Sheets remain flexible. See the full 2026 comparison.

How is a decision-making app different from a pros/cons list?

Pros/cons lists treat every item as equally weighted: "has free parking" counts the same as "doubles my salary." A real decision-making app forces you to quantify how much one criterion matters relative to another, then ranks options by total weighted score. That single change usually flips which option actually wins.

What is AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process)?

A multi-criteria decision analysis method developed by Thomas Saaty in 1977. You compare criteria and options two at a time on a 1-to-9 scale; eigenvector math derives the priority weights; a consistency ratio flags when your own pairwise judgments contradict each other. Used by NASA, Boeing, the World Bank, and Fortune 500 companies for high-stakes calls. Decisio is the consumer-grade implementation.

Does Decisio work offline / privately?

Yes. AI runs on-device via Apple Intelligence on iOS 26+ (no data leaves your phone), or you can plug in your own OpenAI API key for cloud generation. Decisions sync via your own iCloud account through Core Data + CloudKit. No third-party server stores your decision content.

Decisio vs Excel / Google Sheets — which is better for decisions?

Spreadsheets are infinitely flexible but require you to design the scoring model yourself. That's where most people quietly slip back into "I'll just weight things by feel," which defeats the point. A purpose-built decision-making app like Decisio enforces the methodology (pairwise comparison, consistency check, eigenvector priority) so you can't accidentally weight everything equally.

How long does a decision in Decisio take?

5–10 minutes for a typical 3-options × 4-criteria decision in Simple Scoring mode. AHP pairwise mode adds ~3 minutes for a typical decision (you'll do roughly 6 criterion comparisons and ~12 option comparisons). Most users save the recipe in the Playbook and reuse it for similar future decisions.

What happens after I decide? Does Decisio track outcomes?

Yes. Each decision can be marked Right Call / Mixed / Wrong Call weeks or months later. After 5+ rated outcomes you get a personal Calibration Score — your decision-making accuracy percentage. Recipes (what worked) and Principles (lessons learned) accumulate in the Playbook so future decisions inherit your past judgment.
